## Frozen methodological distinction

L2-v1 instantiates the following separation:

```text
implemented
    != available
    != authorized
    != observed
```

For this protocol:

- **implemented** means the downstream W/C/P/E machinery exists in the frozen scientific implementation;
- **available** means the declared downstream transition is technically present within the assay architecture;
- **authorized** means the preregistered causal gate has been satisfied so the protocol permits the downstream branch to execute and become evidentially interpretable;
- **observed** means the authorized downstream transition actually occurred in the scientific execution.

The one-shot specimen reached the frozen candidate universe and fixed trigger, but the discrepancy gate did not open:

```text

W   = NOT_EVALUATED
C   = NOT_EVALUATED

```

Equivalently:

```text

        ↓

```

where `NE` means `NOT_EVALUATED`.

Therefore the downstream machinery receives no evidential credit merely because it was implemented and technically reachable in code.

A compact methodological statement is:

> **The code was capable of continuing. The experiment was not authorized to continue.**

This is a property of the frozen protocol execution, not a general claim about software or experimental governance.

## Frozen estimand distinction

L2-v1 also makes explicit a distinction between a prospective exposure-incidence question and an existential search question.

For a prospectively declared specimen/exposure distribution, the incidence object is of the form:

```text
Pr(D = 1 | frozen exposure rule)
```

This is not the same object as:

```text
1[ exists x in X such that D(x) = 1 ]
```

The first asks how often a preregistered exposure rule opens the discrepancy gate under a declared sampling design. The second asks whether some convenient discrepancy exists somewhere in the domain.

Outcome-conditioned probing can answer the second question while masquerading as the first. L2-v1 therefore forbids replacing a non-discrepant frozen trigger with another probe merely to reach W/C/P/E.

In the realized v1 specimen, the fixed trigger produced:

```text

target(trigger) = 1

```

and the protocol stopped.

## Prospective successor shape — not opened

A scientifically legitimate successor could ask a new question such as:

> **Under a prospectively frozen specimen distribution, what is the incidence of the prerequisite discrepancy gate?**

Such an L2-v2 object would require a new preregistration that fixes, before seeing its specimens:

- the specimen/exposure distribution;
- the number of independent specimens or exact stopping rule;
- the gate-incidence estimand and decision rule;
- whether any `D=1` specimen may open W/C/P/E or whether a fixed number is required;
- how repeated specimens are handled;
- the exact relationship between gate-incidence analysis and conditional W/C/P/E analysis.

W/C/P/E must remain a **predeclared conditional branch**, not the reason for choosing or extending the specimen distribution.

No such successor is authorized by this note.
